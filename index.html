Laser files

Cut everything from the same thickness and glue the frame to the base. Inside the window: the pieces come from the window's offcut and the fit comes from the kerf (clearance isn't used). In RDWorks the layers should come in as blue PIECES, red CUT (window, frame outside), magenta BASE; if not, reorder them so blue cuts before the red window line frees the block. One block below: clearance is added round the window. Separate pieces are kerf-corrected to true size.
